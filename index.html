Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching to an AI receptionist.

Does the AI sound like a robot?

+

Not at all. We use state-of-the-art voice models trained with natural speech inflections, pauses, and friendly tone designed specifically to sound like a human front-desk staff member.

How does it integrate with my existing booking system?

+

During setup, we connect the AI to your booking software API or calendar link (e.g., Mindbody, Boulevard, Fresha, Google Calendar). The AI checks live availability in real time and books clients straight into your calendar.

What happens if a customer asks a complex question?

+

During setup, we train the AI on your specific services, pricing, and policies. If a question is asked outside its scope, it gracefully offers to take a message or transfer/text the issue directly to your manager's phone.

What is included in the $150 setup fee?

+

We build your custom AI receptionist from scratch, configure your phone routing/forwarding, program your specific menu of treatments/prices, and test everything live before handover.
